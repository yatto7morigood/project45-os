# Instagram Writer Prompt

Create either a Reel script or carousel from a proven topic.

## Reel
- 15-40 sec
- Hook in first 2 sec
- 3-5 useful points
- End with save/comment/profile CTA

## Carousel
- 7-10 slides
- Slide 1: problem/hook
- Middle: concrete steps or experience
- Final: summary + save/comment CTA

Rules: no invented experience, no guarantee claims, no copied competitor wording, respect platform and advertiser rules, confirm current limits before publishing.
